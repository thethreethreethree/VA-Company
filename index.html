Common questions

Things merchants ask before signing.

If we haven't answered it here, ask us on the call. We give you a real answer, not a brochure.

How fast can you actually start?

Most stores go live within 72 hours of signing. Complex catalogs or multi-region operations may take up to 5 business days. We never start until your SOPs are signed off — speed without accuracy isn't speed.

Will your team sound like my brand?

Yes — that's the entire point. During onboarding we capture your tone, your vocabulary, your "yes/no" words, and your forbidden phrases. We review past tickets you handled personally and pattern-match. By week two, your most loyal customer won't be able to tell the difference.

What if a VA quits or goes on leave?

Every store gets a named backup from day one. They sit in the same Slack channel, see the same tickets, and know your SOPs. Coverage never drops — that's a contractual promise, not a hope.

Do you work with non-Shopify stores?

Our deepest expertise is Shopify and Shopify Plus, but our team also supports WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and Amazon Seller Central. If you sell across multiple platforms, ask us — we'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.

What about data security?

NDAs are signed by every team member before they see your store. We operate on least-privilege access, use password managers for all credentials, and run on encrypted, audited workstations. We can sign your DPA on request.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month after the first 30 days. If we're not earning our keep, we don't deserve the retainer — and we'd rather you tell us than ghost us.
